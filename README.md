## Course Objectives
This course aims to provide a general foundation in computer graphics. It covers core topics such as modeling, lighting, material application, texturing, animation, rendering, video editing, and the creation of interactive 3D applications. All concepts are demonstrated using Blender, a specialized and cutting-edge graphics software.

## Learning Outcomes
##### Knowledge and Understanding
- Understand the general concepts underlying computer graphics.
- Know key geometric primitives used in computer graphics.
- Understand geometric transformation concepts.
- Learn techniques for modeling graphical elements.
- Understand different color models.
- Understand the basic concepts of visualization.
- Be familiar with discrete techniques for accessing the framebuffer or other buffers.
- Understand fundamental concepts of texture mapping.
- Understand differences between local and global lighting models, and know examples of each.
- Grasp the basic principles of lighting and material application.

##### Application of Knowledge
- Build 3D graphical applications involving modeling, visualization, lighting, materials, textures, rendering, animation, and 3D interaction.
##### Judgment and Evaluation
- Compare publicly available documents and models to assess their usefulness in solving graphical problems.
- Analyze a graphics-related problem and identify solution options.
##### Decision Making
- Choose between different approaches to solving a graphics problem.
- Decide how to graphically present the developed solutions.
##### Communication
- Present and explain the work carried out.
- Clearly respond to questions.
##### Self-Learning Skills
- Learn autonomously in the field of computer graphics.
- Adapt specialized techniques to create graphical solutions.
- Develop projects with open-ended solutions, implementing optional features through research beyond class content.

## Course Contents
##### Theoretical Component
- Introduction to computer graphics
- 3D world geometry
- Vertex meshes, curves, and surfaces
- Modeling techniques
- Visualization
- Color
- Lighting and materials
- Discrete techniques
- 3D interaction
##### Lab Component
- Blender interface
- Modeling
- Lighting
- Material usage
- Animation
- Rendering
- Interaction
